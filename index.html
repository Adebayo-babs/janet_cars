Questions

Do I need a charging station?

No. A normal 220V wall socket or a generator is enough, and the solar roof adds charge in daylight.

What happens on a long trip?

On the Alpha, switch on the fuel extender. Total range goes up to 500 km.

Will the battery handle the heat?

K-Mobile uses lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) cells, which stay stable at high temperatures and last more than 3,000 charge cycles.

Who services the car?

Servicing is free for the first year, anywhere in Nigeria, through the authorised dealer network.

Can my company order several?

Yes. Pick "Fleet" in the booking form and we'll prepare a proposal.
